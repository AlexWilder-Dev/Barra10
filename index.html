I

The kitchen

Exceptional ingredients, handled well and cooked simply. Here, that comes down to one thing above all else: charcoal.

Chef patron Dani Cives has cooked for more than twenty-five years, in Argentina, Mexico, Italy and the UK. At Barra10 he works an open grill fed with wood and charcoal. It’s where the steaks and the octopus get their char.

The menu carries both sides of the family. Argentine steak and empanadas from his; ceviche and tacos from the Mexican side. His co-owner, Marion, runs the room.
