La familia

Who’s cooking

Barra10 is Dani Cives’s place. He’s Argentinian and has been cooking for more than 25 years, in Argentina, Mexico, Italy and now the UK.

He runs it with his wife, who is Mexican, which is why ceviche and tacos sit on the menu next to the steak.

The grill burns wood and charcoal. That’s where the steaks and the octopus get their char.
