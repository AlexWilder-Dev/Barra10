Our story

Fire, family and a market counter.

Barra10 is run by a husband-and-wife team: an Argentinian chef and his Mexican wife. That’s why you’ll find ceviche and tacos next to the steaks.

The heart of the place is an open wood-and-charcoal grill. Chef-patron Dani Cives has spent more than 25 years cooking across Argentina, Mexico, Italy and the UK, and grilling over charcoal is what he does best.

Pull up a seat at the counter or grab a table, and we’ll take it from there.
